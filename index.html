01 / A COMMUNITY BUILT BY PEOPLEOPEN TO EVERYONE
01 — WHY WE EXIST

Talent is everywhere.
Opportunity grows when we share it.

Build for Andhra brings learners, developers, designers, researchers, mentors, and makers into one welcoming space. We believe progress happens when people learn from one another and turn knowledge into things others can use.

Our roots are in Andhra Pradesh. Our community and collaborations can reach anywhere.

04 — HOW WE SHOW UP

Open by default.
Kind by design.

A healthy community is something we build through our everyday actions.

01
Everyone starts somewhere.

Welcome beginners, respect different backgrounds, and make room for questions.

02
Share credit and knowledge.

Recognize collaborators, document decisions, and help others learn independently.

03
Build responsibly.

Respect privacy, accessibility, licensing, safety, and the people affected by our work.

04
Keep it community-led.

Be transparent, invite feedback, and make participation meaningful rather than exclusive.
